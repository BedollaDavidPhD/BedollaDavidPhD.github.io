About

From robot models and controllers to reliable behavior on physical hardware.

I develop robotics controls and motion-generation systems for physical robots, working across trajectory planning, motion control, whole-body coordination, robot modeling, real-time implementation, and hardware validation.

At Lab INIT Robots, I develop a 400 Hz ROS 2 and C++ whole-body control system for a physical 10-DoF AgileX and Kinova Gen3 mobile manipulator. The system combines Cartesian and world-frame tracking, analytical and Jacobian-based redundancy resolution, coordinated arm-base motion, trajectory execution, teleoperation, joint-limit handling, command filtering, and safety-oriented control.

My earlier work on the physical 7-DoF ETS-MARSE rehabilitation exoskeleton included a 1-4 kHz LabVIEW RT/FPGA and C++ motion-control stack, trajectory generation and tracking, force-aware interaction, sensing and actuation, human-oriented inverse kinematics, Gaussian Process-based predictive control, and physical validation.

My completed robot-learning work includes Gaussian Process predictive control, learning-based inverse kinematics, and PyTorch reinforcement-learning experiments in simulation. Teleoperation and multimodal sensing also provide a foundation for future collection of robot and human demonstrations.

Learning-Based Robust Predictive Control on ETS-MARSE

A Gaussian Process models residual dynamics and uncertainty for constrained tracking on the physical exoskeleton.

Technical problem
Track constrained exoskeleton motion despite nonlinear dynamics, model error, and human-robot interaction forces.
My contribution
