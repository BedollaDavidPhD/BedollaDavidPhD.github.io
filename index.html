About

Building reliable robot manipulation from models, sensing, and real-time software.

I am a robotics software engineer with applied R&D experience across the full path from task-level commands and mathematical models to reliable motion on physical robots. I design kinematics and dynamics algorithms, deterministic motion and control software, sensor and hardware interfaces, and validation workflows using C++, ROS 2, Pinocchio, LabVIEW RT and FPGA, MATLAB and Simulink, and Python.

At Lab INIT Robots, I develop a real-time ROS 2 and C++ whole-body control system for a 10-DoF mobile manipulator with a Kinova Gen3 arm. The 400 Hz pipeline coordinates Cartesian motion, arm and base control, redundancy resolution, trajectory execution, teleoperation, joint-limit handling, and safety-oriented pose and velocity commands on physical hardware.

I have also exercised whole-body control in Gazebo and MuJoCo, completed PyTorch reinforcement-learning experiments in simulation, and adapted contact-data interfaces between simulators. These simulation results are presented separately from my physical robot deployments.

My current development direction is Learning from Demonstration for manipulation. Completed work in teleoperation, IMU, EMG, and depth-camera motion acquisition, cross-simulator contact modeling, and model-based execution provides the engineering foundation for future data-collection and policy-execution pipelines. I have not yet trained or deployed an imitation-learning policy.
