About

How I take a control idea to the robot

My work connects kinematics and dynamics to what an operator needs the robot to do. I define the motion objective, fit it to the available command interfaces, and test the resulting behavior on the system.

At Lab INIT Robots, I adapted my earlier swivel-angle approach to joint-velocity control for an AgileX base and Kinova Gen3 arm. I specified the joystick workflow, reviewed AI-generated C++ and integrated and tested the ROS 2 system on the robot.

During my PhD, I implemented and tested predictive controllers on the 7-DoF ETS-MARSE exoskeleton. I used a Gaussian process to select an elbow posture and combined wearable EMG and IMU acquisition for mirror-rehabilitation experiments.

I also build and evaluate robotics tools, including a desktop trajectory planner, symbolic dynamics software, and an embedded manipulator emulator that lets an external controller drive a simulated plant through physical I/O.

World-Frame Mobile Manipulation

One joystick controls the base and arm. In world-frame mode, the arm compensates as the base turns.

Technical problem
Maintain a world-frame end-effector pose while the mobile base moves.
My contribution
Designed the joystick workflow and odometry-based target conversion, reviewed AI-generated C++, and integrated the ROS 2 system. I resolved a conflict between competing arm command publishers.
Hardware
Physical AgileX base with a Kinova Gen3 arm.
Stack
ROS 2, C++, and Pinocchio.
Validation
Observed target compensation during base rotation and residual offset after extended motion. The launch example sets three ROS 2 publishers to 400 Hz and the base-command publisher to 120 Hz.

Analytical Whole-Body Control for a 10-DoF Mobile Manipulator

A simulated task-priority study coordinates the seven arm joints with three base coordinates.

Technical problem
Coordinate arm self-motion, base displacement, and Cartesian tracking in one 10-DoF system.
My contribution
Extended the analytical redundancy formulation with a task-priority hierarchy for end-effector, elbow, and mobile-base objectives.
Simulation
Kinova Gen3 and AgileX mobile-manipulator model.
Stack
Analytical kinematics and task-priority whole-body control.
Validation
Explored end-effector tracking and elbow posture during simulated arm and base motion.

Human-Centered Mirror Rehabilitation on ETS-MARSE

Wearable sensors capture motion on one arm to generate a reference for the exoskeleton on the other.

Technical problem
Turn intermittent motion measurements into smooth joint references for mirror exercises.
My contribution
Combined EMG and IMU acquisition in C++ and integrated the mirror workflow with swivel-angle inverse kinematics and real-time trajectory tracking.
Hardware
Physical 7-DoF ETS-MARSE upper-limb exoskeleton and wearable sensors.
Stack
LabVIEW RT/FPGA and C++.
Validation
The mirror experiment used a 1 ms LabVIEW Real-Time period and a 50 μs FPGA current-loop period.

Robust and Learning-Based Predictive Control on ETS-MARSE

Two physical experiments evaluated robust predictive control and predictive control with a learned model of residual dynamics.

Technical problem
Track constrained exoskeleton motion despite nonlinear dynamics, model error, and human-robot interaction forces.
My contribution
Implemented constrained MPC with integral super-twisting compensation and tested a separate Gaussian process residual model for learning-based MPC.
Hardware
Physical 7-DoF ETS-MARSE rehabilitation exoskeleton.
Stack
Gaussian Processes, model predictive control, C++, and LabVIEW RT.
Validation
The thesis reports 387 μs of computation for 10-step robust MPC and 687 μs for the separate learning-based MPC experiment.

Planning and simulation

Trajectory planning and simulated contact

PlannerGUI for URDF-Based Trajectory Planning

A C++/Qt desktop tool plans and previews joint and Cartesian trajectories for a Kinova Gen3 model.

Technical problem
Generate joint trajectories from Cartesian waypoints and show where the inverse kinematics fails to converge.
My contribution
Designed the URDF-driven workflow, specified separate path and timing stages, and reviewed AI-generated C++. A run that showed 100% progress with failed samples led me to require separate convergence counts and warnings.
Simulation
Kinova Gen3 robot model in a desktop environment.
Stack
C++, Qt, URDF, OpenGL, and Ruckig.
Validation
In a logged desktop run, all 5,001 samples converged for a five-second path sampled offline at a requested 1 kHz. Trajectory generation took 84.93 ms.

Digital-signal-controller robot emulator

During my master's research I developed a TI F28377S manipulator emulator. It exchanges torque inputs and joint-position outputs over analog I/O with an external controller.

Published plant update periods of 10 μs with Euler and 30 μs with fourth-order Runge-Kutta. Read the HIL paper
