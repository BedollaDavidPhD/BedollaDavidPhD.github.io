Controller tuning

Dynamics Forge

The browser simulator uses nonlinear articulated-body dynamics with RK4 integration at 1 kHz and a 250 Hz controller. Rotor systems also include thrust and drag, motor delay and limits, encoder quantization, state estimation, and direct allocation. Initial integral values and antiwindup limits are percentages of the fixed actuator stall input, before velocity and power constraints. Default gains are deliberately untuned starting values with visible room for improvement. Modify them and compare tracking and effort.
